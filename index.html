A note from the maker

I built this because paper business cards end up in a drawer. Link-in-bio pages all look the same.

When you pick a trainer, a realtor or a tattoo artist, you're really picking a person. Fifteen seconds of their face and voice tells you more than a logo ever will. So that's the whole idea: a card that says hello, and you still don't have to film anything.

How it works

Three steps. About five minutes.

  1. Upload a selfie.

    One clear, front-facing photo. Add a short voice sample if you want the video to sound like you.

    kitchen lighting is fine, really

  2. Write two or three sentences.

    Who you are, who you help and what people should do next. Then pick the languages your clients speak.

  3. Share the card.

    You get your own link and QR code with call, email, booking and social buttons, plus the talking video.

Questions people ask

FAQ

Do I need to film anything?

No. One selfie is enough, and the AI animates it into a short talking video. If you'd rather use real footage, you can upload your own clip instead.

Will it really sound like me?

On Pro, you record a short voice sample and the intro is made in your own voice, even in languages you don't speak. You can also pick a stock voice.

Which languages?

The plan is to cover the major languages today's voice and video AI supports, including English, Finnish, Swedish, Spanish, German and French. The final list will be confirmed at launch.

What happens to my face and voice?

They're used only to make your own videos, and you can delete them anytime. No one gets a video of you without your consent.

Does the other person need an app?

No. A TalkCard is a normal web page. It opens in any phone browser from a link, a QR code or an NFC tag.

Can I cancel?

Anytime. It's monthly, with no lock-in.
